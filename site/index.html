How it works

Intelligent airdrop: completed tasks are scored by public completion, quality, and speed criteria. Open grants: proposals are evaluated by impact, feasibility, openness, and community benefit. Investment DAO: paper proposals are checked for quorum, majority, risk, and concentration limits.
